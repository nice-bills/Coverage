# Product

Coverage is a static, self-contained research dashboard (`dist/index.html`, generated by
`dashboard.py`) for one question: how much of NEAR's token issuance is matched by fee burn and
NEAR Intents buybacks, and how far is break-even.

register: product

## Who reads it

Hackathon judges (crypto investors, protocol founders) and automated screeners. An analyst at a
desk in daylight, checking a claim against its evidence, with the repo open in the next tab.

## What it must do

- State the finding in one sentence and show it to scale before any chart.
- Put the source and the finding next to every figure.
- Name each model, the function that implements it, and the command that reproduces it.
- Show the frozen forecast rounds and their live score.
- Give the case against the same weight as the case for.

## Voice

Plain, specific, measured. Numbers with units. No promotion.

## Design

An auditor's working paper under daylight: black ink, ruled lines, one teal for what comes back
to the token and one orange for what is burnt. No cards, no external assets, system fonts, light
and dark. Tokens live at the top of `dashboard.py`.
