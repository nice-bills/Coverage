# Coverage demo film: brief

FILM      The one sentence a viewer keeps: NEAR wins back 12%, not 1%, of what it mints, and you can check every number yourself.
          Audience: NEAR hackathon judges (crypto investors, protocol founders). 72 s, 1920x1080, 30 fps. Works muted (captions carry it).
ASSETS    Real screens only, captured from dist/ (film/assets/*.png); real run.py output (film/assets/run_output.txt);
          numbers from out/results.json via film/build.py. Fonts and colours from dashboard.py: Instrument Serif, Inter,
          JetBrains Mono; paper #F5F0E6, ink #1D1B17, teal #008C80 (comes back), terracotta #C4532D (burnt).
VISUAL    Editorial, left-aligned, like the dashboard. Banned: centred title on a gradient, everything fading in, glow.
          Something new on screen every 2 to 4 s. Motion: springs; UI snaps, panels settle, headlines land and hold.
HARD LINE Never invent a screen or a metric. Every number on screen is in out/results.json or the dashboard.
DELIVER   film/out/coverage-demo.mp4, a poster frame, a contact sheet in film/reviews/.
